FirstThought / Case study

From days to hours: streamlining expert research

01 Summary02 Problem03 What we learned04 Key decision05 Design06 Outcome07 Reflection

01 / Summary

Founding Member & Product Design Lead · 2019–2022

I owned the roadmap and identity and was the sole designer. The team included two engineers, compliance and partnerships, and research and business leads.

02 / Problem

Research Managers assembled expert evidence one source and one page at a time. Collecting and organizing information took time away from evaluating expert fit and working with clients.

03 / What we learned

A JavaScript scraper prototype exposed the repetitive work: visit a clinical-trial page, extract text, structure useful fields, and identify potential experts.

At Quality Reviews, an earlier startup of mine, texts had drawn stronger responses than email. That experience gave us a hypothesis to test when expert outreach became the next bottleneck.

04 / Key decision

Build around the next constraint: CRM → discovery → email → SMS. Expert records came first; automated discovery filled them; email connected communications; SMS addressed the difficulty of hearing back.

The sequence kept each phase tied to the research team’s current work.

05 / Design

06 / Outcome

Vetting went from days to hours for a 25-person team handling 7,500+ expert candidates annually. SMS lifted response rates 1.5×, across 2,000+ messages over a few months.

LifeSci Partners announced a strategic partnership in September 2021, making FirstThought’s expert-network services available across its advisory and consulting businesses. Read the partnership announcement ↗

07 / Reflection

Building from zero taught me to make uncertainty concrete. A small scraper gave us evidence to discuss, and an ordered roadmap made that evidence actionable. Understanding services such as Power Automate and Twilio also helped me shape what we could build with a small team.
